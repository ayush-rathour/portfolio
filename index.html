WHO I AM

Curious mind.

I'm Ayush Rathour - a student developer from Saharanpur, India, passionate about building modern, user-focused web experiences that combine clean design, performance, and functionality.

My journey into web development began at 14 with a simple HTML page built out of curiosity. What started as small experiments quickly grew into building complete websites, solving real-world problems, and turning ideas into functional digital experiences.

Through personal projects, freelance work, and continuous learning, I've developed a passion for crafting clean, purposeful, and engaging web experiences.
